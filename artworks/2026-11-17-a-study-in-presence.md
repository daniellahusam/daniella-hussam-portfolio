  Details

  Size: 30 x 40 x 2 cm

  Rarity: Reference artwork for the photographer Rudi Huisman

  Medium: Mixed Media on canvas

  Year: 2021


  About the work.

  This painting began with a photograph by Rudi Huisman. Something about the
  woman's presence, her posture and her quiet expression really resonated with
  me.


  I felt drawn to translate that feeling into my own visual language.


  Rather than simply recreating the photograph, I wanted to reinterpret it
  through colour, texture, expressive brushwork and my own perspective.


  And somewhere between the original image and my interpretation, the painting
  found its own identity.


  *Had been exhibited in Calle Valencia 17, 28012 Madrid, December 4th-2026
