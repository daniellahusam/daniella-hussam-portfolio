medium: Recycled wood, banners
description: >
  3 Meter installation sculpture made of recycled materials

  exhibited at Ras Al Ain gallery-recycled art festival symposium
   
  We all have what’s called a ‘’comfort zone’’.

  The comfort zone is a logical or a psychological space where you feel
  comfortable. Everyone has a different line in the sand. Some will stay near
  the shore while others will swim in the deep.

  However, if we cross our line in the sand and fight against our fear we all
  can grow.

  Stand in the middle of this sculpture and think what your line in the sand is?
