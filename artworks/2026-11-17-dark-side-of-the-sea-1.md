---
title: Dark side of the sea
category: Installations
year: "2020"
medium: wood, clay, resin, found objects
description: >-
  Installation pieces contain drawers, dolphin's fluke, toy box, bed hanging
  from the roof filled with childish drawings of fears and lost dreams.

  This project discussed child abuse and sexual harassment.

  The ocean represents human nature, or man, because the apparent or obvious
  side of human nature shares our moral values, but when we dive deeper into the
  human soul as our ocean diving, the chaotic, extraordinary dimensions that we
  dissemble and try to posses.


  *Had been exhibited at Dar art fair, Sweifieh village, Amman, Jordan, July 1st
  2021
