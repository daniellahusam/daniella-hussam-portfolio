  About the work

  Wanderlust is the peak of my exploration of the theme "the

  flaws in art criticism." I’ve faced criticism for choosing abstract,

  “cartoonish” objects, inspired by my work as an art teacher. Working with

  children, I saw how they create without fear of mistakes, which opened

  my eyes to new perspectives. I realised that this fearless creativity is key

  to growth as an artist. Despite challenges in being accepted due to a

  perceived lack of "skills," I believe art isn't just about technique. It’s
  about

  how we view the world and create experiences for others. And so, I'll

  continue to wander."

  *Had been exhibited in 2 different exhibitions

  1. Mini exhibition at languages city, Amman, Jordan, August 22nd 2024

  2. Not your grandma's exhibition, 101 Reade St. New York, USA, March 20th 2026
