  About the work

  ''The fool'' began as a self-portrait, but it slowly became a collection of
  memories, emotions, and symbols.

  every element has its own story. Some are deeply personal, others are open to
  interpretation. The clown, the candle, the balloons, the floating eyes, the
  little yellow duck...each one represents a different chapter, emotion, or
  memory that found its way into the work.

  Symbolism doesn't ask for a single answer. Once a painting is finished, it
  starts a conversation, and no two interpretations are ever the same.
