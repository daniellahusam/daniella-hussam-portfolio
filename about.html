About the artist

A little context about the person, ideas and practice behind the work.

Daniella Hussam · Visual artist

Working between
image, material and idea.

Daniella Hussam is a visual artist and art educator whose practice moves across painting, mixed media and material experimentation. Her work explores colour, identity, emotion and surreal imagery, often bringing personal symbolism and playful visual language into conversation.

Alongside her studio practice, Daniella works in art education and is interested in the ways creative research, experimentation and material processes shape an artwork from its earliest studies to its final form.
