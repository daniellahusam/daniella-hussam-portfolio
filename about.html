Daniella Husam · Visual artist

Working between image, material and idea.

Daniella Husam, also known as Dania Husam, is a visual artist and art educator whose practice moves across painting, mixed media and material experimentation. Her work explores colour, identity, emotion and surreal imagery, often bringing personal symbolism and playful visual language into conversation.

Alongside her studio practice, Daniella is interested in the ways creative research, experimentation and material processes shape an artwork from its earliest studies to its final form.
